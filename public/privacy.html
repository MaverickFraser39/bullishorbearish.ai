Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party requests. This page describes the little that is collected and why.

What is collected

  • What you write. The company names, descriptions and takes you submit, plus the display name you optionally attach. All of it is public by design — that is the point of the site.
  • An anonymous vote fingerprint. To keep one person from voting fifty times, your IP address and browser user-agent are combined with a secret salt and hashed with SHA-256. Only that hash is stored. Your IP address is never written to the database, and the hash cannot be reversed to recover it.
  • Ordinary server logs held briefly by the hosting provider (Fly.io) for operational purposes.

What is not collected

  • No accounts, no email addresses, no passwords.
  • No cookies and no advertising or analytics trackers of any kind.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts are served from this domain, so no font CDN or any other outside party sees your visit.

Legal basis and retention

Under UK and EU data protection law the vote fingerprint is processed on the basis of legitimate interest — specifically, preventing ballot stuffing on a public board. It is the least identifying mechanism that achieves that; the alternative would be requiring accounts, which collects far more.

Submissions are kept until removed. Rotating the salt permanently detaches every existing fingerprint from any person.

Your rights

You can ask for content you submitted to be removed, or ask what is held about you. Because posting is anonymous, we may be unable to link a request to specific entries unless you can identify them. Reach us through GitHub issues.

An honest limitation

The vote fingerprint is a speed bump, not a wall. Anyone with a VPN or a second browser can vote more than once. Treat every number on this site as directional, never authoritative.
